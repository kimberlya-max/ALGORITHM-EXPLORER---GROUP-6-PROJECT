# Proposed Work Split for a Five-Member Group

This is a planning guide for dividing the remaining review, verification, and submission work. It gives two members larger ownership areas and assigns three smaller, concrete tasks so everyone has a clear way to contribute.

**Important:** The effort shares below are planning estimates, not a record of work already completed and not a substitute for the assignment's contribution history. Do not claim that someone implemented or reviewed work unless they actually did so. Since much of the application and report has already been prepared, members taking an existing task should perform and record a real review, correction, or verification rather than receive retroactive credit for the original implementation.

## Suggested allocation

| Placeholder | Planned share of remaining effort | Main responsibility | Required output |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Member 1 — Primary lead | 30% | Review the algorithm engine: sorting behavior, recursion results, data-structure operations, counters, validation, and edge cases. Own any fixes found during review. | A short review log naming the files and cases checked, any fixes made, and results from the relevant engine tests. |
| Member 2 — Primary lead | 30% | Verify benchmark reproducibility and analysis; integrate final application evidence and report edits; coordinate the final submission package. | Rechecked benchmark data and interpretation, final report/PDF review, and a submission checklist with unresolved items identified. |
| Member 3 — Supporting contributor | 13.33% | Cross-check the assignment brief against the application and report. | A requirement-to-evidence checklist identifying where each requirement is met or what remains incomplete. |
| Member 4 — Supporting contributor | 13.33% | Run a manual walkthrough of the four modules and execute the test suite. Record any defects rather than only reporting “works.” | Test log with environment, steps, pass/fail results, and screenshots or issue descriptions for failures. |
| Member 5 — Supporting contributor | 13.34% | Check documentation and submission readiness: references, README instructions, report placeholders, repository link, and final file naming. | A completed proofreading/submission checklist and a list of any fields the group still needs to supply. |

The shares total 100% of the **remaining planned effort**. Adjust them if the actual tasks or availability change; never copy them into the final contribution record as though they were measured historical contributions.

## Suggested sequence

1. Replace the role placeholders with the five members' names and agree on a due date.
2. The two primary leads divide the core review and final integration work; each supporting member completes the assigned checklist and returns evidence to a lead.
3. Members report blockers early. Reassign unfinished tasks openly and record who ultimately completed each one.
4. Have one person who did not prepare a particular section verify it, especially benchmark values, report claims, and test results.
5. Before submission, confirm that the report's group number, member names, registration numbers, actual contributions, signatures, and repository URL are accurate and complete.
